A collective built on relationships, not press releases.

We started E2W Collective because too many talented people were handing their story to agencies that treated every client the same way. A boutique hotel needs a different kind of press than a film premiere. A debut album needs a different timeline than a chef's first tasting menu, and we plan for that difference from the very first call.

Our team has placed clients in outlets that cover film, music, food and design, and we know the editors who actually read a well-timed pitch. We work in New York and Los Angeles, on set, backstage and in the kitchen, wherever the story is actually happening.
