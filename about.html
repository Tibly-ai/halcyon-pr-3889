About the collective

We shape the stories our clients are known for.

Halcyon PR Collective was built on a simple belief: coverage should feel inevitable, not accidental. We work with filmmakers, musicians, restaurateurs, and hospitality groups who need press coverage that lands in the right outlets, framed the right way, at the right moment.

Our mission

We started this agency because entertainment PR had gotten lazy. Blast a press release, hope an editor bites, repeat. That approach might get a mention. It rarely builds a reputation.

Our job is to understand what makes a client's story true, not just marketable, and to place it with the outlets and voices that make people pay attention. We do that for indie film releases, touring musicians, boutique hotels, and restaurant groups across New York and Los Angeles.

Strategy before outreach

Every campaign starts with a narrative brief, not a media list.

Relationships, kept warm

We pitch editors we've worked with for years, not cold contacts from a database.

Craft over volume

Fewer, sharper placements beat a long list of passing mentions.

"Good press isn't luck. It's a narrative built with intention, handed to the right person at the right time."

Mara Voss, Founder & Chief Strategist
