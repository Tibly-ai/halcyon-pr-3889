Services

Press strategy built for how entertainment actually works.

Halcyon PR Collective works with film, music, and hospitality clients who need coverage that reaches the right desk, the right editor, and the right audience. No templates, no wire blasts. Every service below is built around the client in front of us.

What we do for clients

Six disciplines, one team. Most campaigns pull from three or four of these at once, depending on what the moment calls for.

01

Media Relations & Press Placement

Pitching entertainment trades, lifestyle glossies, and digital outlets to land features, reviews, and interviews that reach the audience that matters.

02

Campaign Strategy & Launches

Building the press timeline around a film release, album drop, restaurant opening, or hotel launch so coverage lands exactly when it should.

03

Brand Positioning & Messaging

Writing the narrative, talking points, and bio material that keep talent, founders, and executives consistent across every interview.

04

Red Carpet, Junkets & Premieres

Running press lines, managing junket schedules, and coordinating premiere logistics so the story stays on the work, not the backstage chaos.

05

Crisis & Reputation Management

Drafting response language and coordinating outreach fast when a story breaks the wrong way, protecting standing with press and public alike.

06

Influencer & Partnership Strategy

Matching brands with collaborators whose audience and tone actually fit, then handling outreach and agreements from first contact to signed deal.
